# Sock Fighters: LOST IN THE SPIN CYCLE
*Adventure mode: plan and story script (draft 3, for review)*

---

## 1. What it plays like
- **Hero select (the League-style part):** a big, dramatic screen. Hero portraits run along the side; the chosen hero stands huge in the middle with their name, title and a funny one-liner. Their abilities are shown as icons you can tap to read. Skins (including your Dryer styles) sit along the bottom, and there's a big **LOCK IN** button. Locking in plays the hero's catchphrase and a dramatic whoosh.
- **Exploring:** the camera looks down at an angle at the hero. You **tap or click where to walk**, and tap enemies to attack them.
- **Abilities:** every hero has a **basic attack** (tap or click an enemy) plus **3 different special abilities** on buttons **1 2 3** (keyboard) or big round buttons (phone), each with its own cooldown. On top of that there's an **ULT** that charges as you fight.
- **Story chapters:** each chapter is a new place with silly enemies, a few secrets, funny story scenes, and a **boss** at the end. Bosses have attacks you can see coming and dodge.
- **Level ups:** you level up during a chapter, and each level makes one special stronger (you pick which).
- **Saving:** chapters you've cleared, and their rewards, save on your device, just like trophies.
- **No** lanes, minion waves or towers. This isn't a League match; it borrows the hero select and the controls.
- Later: **co-op**, with a friend joining online as a second hero.

## 2. The characters

### 👑 QUEEN NYLONIA, the Pantyhose Queen (the one who asks for help)
**ナイロニア女王** · *"Two legs. Zero runs. Infinite elegance."*
- **Who she is:** ruler of **the Hosiery**, a different species from socks: two-legged pantyhose people joined at a fancy waistband. They live on their own island, the **Island of Nylonia**, in the middle of the **Rinse Sea** on the other side of the dryer.
- **Look:** tall and glamorous, with a shimmering lilac sheen, a jewelled crown on her waistband, a long silky train, and a sceptre shaped like a darning needle. Cartoon and all-ages, like the rest of the cast.
- **Personality:** grand, theatrical and wonderfully vain. She calls herself "We" (*"We have TWO LEGS, darling. We are always a 'we'"*). Underneath it she's brave and kind, and she would never admit she's scared.
- **Running joke:** if anything so much as brushes her, she shrieks "WE HAVE A **RUN**!" and her guards rush in with nail varnish.
- **Her role:** she opens the dryer portal and **asks your chosen hero to save both worlds**. Between chapters she's your mission boss back at her palace, and she sends Hosiery helpers along with you.

### Her Hosiery (your allies)
Different styles of pantyhose, each with a job in the Queen's army. Some help you in fights; others run the palace.

| Hosiery | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Seamed Stocking Guards** | The Queen's palace guards; march in perfect lines, very serious |
| **Fishnet Scouts** | Rough, loud explorers; throw nets to hold enemies still for you |
| **Sheer Spies** | Nearly invisible; reveal secret paths and hidden treasure |
| **Control-Top Bodyguards** | Huge and squeezy; block attacks for you |
| **Tights Troopers** | Cosy, bouncy foot soldiers; cheer you on (and sometimes heal you) |
| **Lacetop Ladies** | The Queen's elegant advisers; run the shop for power-ups |
| **Glitter Tights** | Party showoffs; light up dark caves with sparkle |
| **Footless Leggings** | Messengers who are always running (they have no feet to stop with) |

**The twist:** the Demon's heat and static **"tumble-dry" some Hosiery** and turn them against you, so you'll also *fight* static-crazed Fishnets, Sheers and Control-Tops in his territory. Beat them and they snap back to normal: *"...Where am I? Why am I so CRUNCHY?"*

### 🔥 THE DRYER DEMON and his minions (the villains)
He rules the **Tumble Throne**, a burning dryer drum deep under the Rinse Sea. He has been stealing socks from our world, and now stockings from Nylonia too, for reasons nobody knows.

**His generals are the villains you already know from the fighting game:**
| Chapter | General | Their land |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Holey Moley** + the Lint Gang (Lint Bunnies, Socklets) | The Lint Plains |
| 2 | **Cling Queen** | The Static Swamp |
| 3 | **Bleach Baron** | The Bleach Badlands |
| 4 | **Iron Shogun** with **Steel Toe** and **King Knee-Sock** | The Ironing Fortress |
| 5 | **THE DRYER DEMON** | The Tumble Throne |

### The heroes (you)
The hero you lock in is the one the Queen calls: all 8 heroes, plus any villains you've unlocked. The script below uses **Stinky Tabi**; if you pick someone else, they say their own version of his lines. Each hero gets a basic attack, **3 different specials** and an ULT. A few examples:

| Hero | Special 1 | Special 2 | Special 3 | ULT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stinky Tabi | Stink-o-ken (stink cloud you throw) | Stink Aura (fumes all around) | Sweat Slide (slippery dash) | Gym Bag Galaxy |
| Stocking Gran | Cane Whack (long stick hit) | Knitting Trap (yarn that slows enemies) | "Back in My Day" (a lecture so long enemies fall asleep) | Grandma's Fury |
| Mecha-Sock X | Rocket Toe | Compression Shield | Laser Lint-Sweep | Full Compression Mode |

## 3. The story

### The big idea
Every laundry day, **one sock goes missing**. It's the greatest mystery of the universe.

The answer: **the Dryer Demon** is taking them, through every dryer in the world. Now he's after the Hosiery of Nylonia too. **Queen Nylonia** can't stop him alone, so she reaches through the dryer and calls for a hero.

---

### PROLOGUE: LAUNDRY DAY
*The coin laundry at midnight. The neon sign flickers. Dryer No. 7 starts spinning by itself. A sock is sucked toward the door.*

**STINKY TABI:** HEY! THAT'S MY OTHER HALF!
**GRAN:** In my day, socks went missing the honest way. Behind the radiator! ...Oh, my back.

*The dryer window glows purple. A glamorous face appears in the glass: QUEEN NYLONIA.*

**QUEEN NYLONIA:** Greetings, single-leggers. We are **Queen Nylonia**, ruler of the Hosiery and of the beautiful **Island of Nylonia**. *(a tiny sparkle)*
**ARGYLE-SAN:** Why is she saying "we"? There is one of her.
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** We have TWO LEGS, darling. We are *always* a "we".
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** Listen carefully, for We will only say this once. *(beat)* ...Twice if you're slow. The **Dryer Demon** has awoken. He steals your socks. He steals Our stockings. Soon there will be nothing left in any drawer, in any world. We need a **hero**.

*Everyone looks at Stinky Tabi. Stinky Tabi looks behind himself.*

**STINKY TABI:** ...Me?
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** *(squinting)* We asked for a hero. Not a... *(sniffs through the glass)* ...gym bag.
**STINKY TABI:** He took my other half, lady. I'm going.
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** *(dramatic pause)* ...Very well. Step into the dryer. Bring snacks. Do NOT touch Our curtains.

**→ Hero select:** the first time the League-style hero select appears. *"Who will answer the Queen's call?"*

**Portal animation:** the dryer door swirls into a glowing portal. The hero tumbles through, spinning past floating buttons, coins and one very confused rubber duck.
**STINKY TABI:** *(spinning)* I REGRET NOTHIIIIING!

### CHAPTER 0: THE PALACE OF NYLONIA (the hub)
*The hero lands in a glittering palace of silk and lace on top of an island. Seamed Stocking Guards march past in perfect lines. Out of the window: the Rinse Sea, and far away, a column of black smoke rising from the water.*

**QUEEN NYLONIA:** Welcome to Nylonia. Please wipe your... *(looks at the hero's foot)* ...everything.
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** That smoke is the **Tumble Throne**, where the Demon sits. To reach it, you must defeat his four generals. We shall send Our finest Hosiery with you.
**FISHNET SCOUT:** *(saluting)* Reporting for duty, Your Majesty!
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** And this is Our finest?
**FISHNET SCOUT:** I've got lots of holes, so I'm very breathable on long trips.

**Gameplay:** the palace is the **hub**. Between chapters you come back here to level up, visit the Lacetop Ladies' shop, change heroes on the hero select screen, and get the Queen's next mission.

---

### CHAPTER 1: THE LINT PLAINS
*A soft grey shore across the sea. Hills of lint. A lint river, flowing very slowly.*

**STINKY TABI:** Ugh. It's like walking on a sweater that gave up.

*A LINT BUNNY hops out from behind a fluff rock.*

**LINT BUNNY:** HALT! You are entering the land of the... *(sneezes)* ...Lint Gang!
**STINKY TABI:** Bless you.
**LINT BUNNY:** *(sniffles)* Nobody ever says bless you. *(sneezes again, and three more lint bunnies fall out)*
**STINKY TABI:** ...Did you just sneeze out your FAMILY?
**LINT BUNNY:** Every lint bunny is born from a sneeze. It's beautiful, actually. *(then, remembering)* ...Get him, family!

**Gameplay:** waves of Lint Bunnies and speedy Socklets, plus lint tumbleweeds rolling in the way.

**Boss: HOLEY MOLEY.** Full of moth holes and very proud of it.
**HOLEY MOLEY:** Nobody passes! The Demon made ME guardian of the plains!
**STINKY TABI:** You're more hole than sock.
**HOLEY MOLEY:** I'm *breathable*.
**FISHNET SCOUT:** *(offended)* Hey. That's MY thing.

*After the fight:*
**HOLEY MOLEY:** The Demon said if I guard the plains, he'd fix my holes. *(a moth flies out of his mouth)* ...He hasn't fixed my holes.
**STINKY TABI:** Why does he even want all these socks?
**HOLEY MOLEY:** Nobody knows. He just keeps saying... "*Not her. Not her. NOT HER.*"

---

### CHAPTER 2: THE STATIC SWAMP
*Purple fog. Crackling sparks. Everything sticks to everything. A balloon floats past, covered in socks.*

*Mecha-Sock X is stuck to a tree by static.*

**MECHA-SOCK X:** GREETINGS. I AM HERE ON A RESCUE MISSION.
**STINKY TABI:** You're stuck to a tree.
**MECHA-SOCK X:** THE RESCUE MISSION IS NOW FOR ME.

*Peeled off, he joins as a guest hero for this chapter.*

**MECHA-SOCK X:** COMPRESSION LEVEL: GRATEFUL.

**Gameplay:** static-crazed (tumble-dried) Sheers and Fishnets pop out of the fog. Beat them to snap them back to normal: *"...Why am I so CRUNCHY?"*

**Boss: CLING QUEEN.** Sassy, glamorous, and furious that there's another queen in town.
**CLING QUEEN:** So, *Nylonia* sent a little errand sock. How sweet.
**STINKY TABI:** You know her?
**CLING QUEEN:** Know her? Darling, we were the two most fabulous royals in the Rinse Sea. Then she got an ISLAND and I got... *(gestures at the swamp)* ...THIS.
**CLING QUEEN:** There's only room for ONE queen, sweetie. And she's wearing last season's sheen.

*After the fight, her static fizzles out.*
**CLING QUEEN:** Ugh. Fine. The Demon pays me to *stick* the stolen socks together and float them to his throne. A girl's gotta get her dry cleaning paid for.
*(back at the palace, later)*
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** *(icily)* ...She said *last season's sheen?*
**STINKY TABI:** She did.
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** We are going to need a moment.

---

### CHAPTER 3: THE BLEACH BADLANDS
*A blinding white desert. Every colour drains away the longer you stand still. Little signs read "NO STAINS BEYOND THIS POINT".*

**Gameplay twist:** standing still too long fades your colours and slows you down, so keep moving and find shade.

*Baby Bootie is crying under a rock.*

**BABY BOOTIE:** I was BLUE! I was SO BLUE! Now I'm... *(sniffles)* ...EGGSHELL!
**TIGHTS TROOPER:** *(hugging Baby)* Aww. Eggshell is very in this year.
**BABY BOOTIE:** *(stops crying)* ...Really?
**TIGHTS TROOPER:** No. But we'll get your blue back.

**Boss: BLEACH BARON.** Spotless, strict and obsessed with cleanliness.
**BLEACH BARON:** Halt, you STAINED creature!
**STINKY TABI:** It's not a stain. It's *character*.
**BLEACH BARON:** That's what EVERY stain says.

*After the fight, colour floods back into the desert like a rainbow.*
**BABY BOOTIE:** I'M BLUE AGAIN!! *(tantrum of joy)*
**BLEACH BARON:** *(sobbing quietly)* I just wanted everything to be... clean... The Demon promised a world with no more mess. No more mismatched socks...
**STINKY TABI:** There's more to life than being spotless, Baron. Sometimes the stains are where the stories are.
**BLEACH BARON:** ...Is that from a fortune cookie?
**STINKY TABI:** It's from ME. *(sniffs himself)* ...I'm basically a fortune cookie at this point.

---

### CHAPTER 4: THE IRONING FORTRESS
*A giant steaming castle shaped like an iron. Conveyor belts carry flattened socks. Everything hisses.*

*Gran is already here. Nobody knows how.*

**STINKY TABI:** GRAN?! How did you get here?
**GRAN:** I took the stairs.
**STINKY TABI:** There are no stairs to another dimension!
**GRAN:** There are if you're old enough. *(cracks back)* ...Ooh. Ow. Right. Let's go.

*Gran joins as a guest hero with her walking stick.*

**Mini-bosses:** **STEEL TOE** ("SAFETY FIRST... except for you!") guards the gate, and **KING KNEE-SOCK** demands everyone kneel. *(Gran: "Young man, if I kneel, I'm not getting back up.")*

**Boss: IRON SHOGUN.** An honourable steam-armour warrior who flattens anything wrinkly.
**IRON SHOGUN:** I am IRON SHOGUN. I press. I flatten. I remove all WRINKLES.
**GRAN:** *(stepping forward)* Young man, I have been wrinkly since before you were a CAST-IRON TODDLER.
**IRON SHOGUN:** ...The grandmother. Even I fear... the grandmother.

*After the fight:*
**IRON SHOGUN:** You fight with honour. The Demon waits at the Tumble Throne. But know this: he does not steal socks out of evil.
**STINKY TABI:** Then WHY?
**IRON SHOGUN:** *(steam sighs out of him)* ...Out of heartbreak.

*Back at the palace:*
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** Heartbreak? *(quietly)* ...We know a little about that. *(then, loudly)* ANYWAY. Go and punch him, please.

---

### CHAPTER 5: THE TUMBLE THRONE
*Deep under the Rinse Sea: a giant spinning drum, scorching hot. In the middle sits a throne made entirely of odd socks and stolen stockings. Thousands of them.*

*The Queen herself comes along for the final battle, with her guards.*

**QUEEN NYLONIA:** We do not normally go places that are this... *damp*. But for Our people, We make an exception.
**STINKY TABI:** *(running in)* MY OTHER HALF! *(hugs a sock)* ...Wait, this isn't him. *(sniffs)* ...This is somebody's GYM sock. Close, though.

**THE DRYER DEMON:** WHO DARES ENTER... THE TUMBLE THRONE?!
**STINKY TABI:** Stinky Tabi. Legend of the Gym Bag.
**THE DRYER DEMON:** HAHAHAHA! I have taken a sock from EVERY laundry day for a THOUSAND YEARS!
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** And Our stockings! Give them back this INSTANT, you overheated appliance!
**THE DRYER DEMON:** ...*(long pause)*... I'm looking for someone.

**Boss: THE DRYER DEMON.** A three-phase fight: fireballs, lint portals, and the floor of the drum spinning beneath you. In the final phase the Queen joins in with Ladder Cascade: *"If We have a run, EVERYONE has a run!"*

*At the end, the Demon's flames flicker down to a tiny, sad pilot light.*

**THE DRYER DEMON:** A thousand years ago... I was just a sock. A plain black sock. I had a partner. Then one laundry day... she was gone.
**STINKY TABI:** *(tearing up)* So you've been taking socks... trying to find your pair?
**THE DRYER DEMON:** Every. Single. One. And none of them are HER.

*A ghostly shimmer. THE LOST SOCK floats in.*

**THE LOST SOCK:** ...Hi.
**THE DRYER DEMON:** ...*Darling?*
**THE LOST SOCK:** I fell down the back of the drawer.
**THE DRYER DEMON:** FOR A THOUSAND YEARS?!
**THE LOST SOCK:** ...It was cozy.

*Silence. Then everyone cheers. The Demon and the Lost Sock hug, and the Throne of Odd Socks explodes into a shower of reunited pairs. Stinky's other half lands right in his arms.*

**STINKY TABI:** *(sniffs)* ...Smells like HOME.
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** *(dabbing her eyes)* This is the most beautiful thing We have ever seen. *(gasps)* ...Oh no.
**STINKY TABI:** What?
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** We cried so hard... *(looks down)* ...WE HAVE A **RUN**.

### EPILOGUE: THE ROYAL LAUNDRY BALL
*Back at the Palace of Nylonia. A grand ball. Socks and Hosiery dance together. The Demon (pilot light glowing happily) is running the cloakroom.*

**QUEEN NYLONIA:** *(to the hero)* For saving both Our worlds, We name you... **Knight of the Royal Drawer**.
**STINKY TABI:** Do I get a crown?
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** You get a *washing*. Immediately.
**THE DRYER DEMON:** Welcome to the cloakroom. Lost socks are returned within one thousand years. *(beat)* ...We're working on that.
**CLING QUEEN:** *(arriving, uninvited)* Love the ball, Nylonia. Shame about the sheen.
**QUEEN NYLONIA:** ...Guards.
**GRAN:** Now can somebody help me down from this chandelier? I don't know how I got up here either.

*Post-credits: a single glove vanishes from a coat pocket somewhere. A tiny voice whispers:*
**???:** "...Gloves are next."

## 4. Build order
1. **Queen Nylonia and the Hosiery**: new two-legged characters (drawing, voices). The Queen could also become an unlockable fighter later.
2. **Hero select screen** (the League-style one) and the **dryer portal** animation.
3. **Adventure engine:** angled top-down view, tap-to-move, a basic attack, 3 specials and an ULT, enemies, bosses, level ups, the palace hub, phone buttons.
4. **Prologue, the palace hub and Chapter 1**, fully playable with story scenes. Then you try it.
5. Chapters 2–5 and the epilogue.
6. Co-op with a friend online.

*All story lines, characters and art are original. Nothing is copied from League of Legends or any other game.*
